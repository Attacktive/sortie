Only the nine tiles actually used are vendored, renamed from their numbered
originals. The full packs are free to download from the links above.

The forest tile is the autumn tree rather than the pine: the pine is green on green, and against a grass tile only its outline survives.

Every tile is 16x16. The grid cell is 48px, so they render at exactly 3x with
nearest-neighbour filtering — integer scaling, no blurring.
